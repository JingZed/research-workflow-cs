# Style Spec Template

## Canvas

- aspect_ratio: <16:9 | 4:3 | custom>
  Match the expected projector, venue, or template format.
- typeface: <font family, fallback, and usage rule>
  Choose readable fonts that the final PPTX environment can support.
- palette: <primary, secondary, accent, neutral, and contrast notes>
  Keep color choices consistent across plots, diagrams, and section slides.

## Visual Treatment

- figure_treatment: <crop, redraw, caption, border, callout, or simplification rules>
  Define how paper figures and generated assets should appear on slides.
- section_divider_rhythm: <none | sparse | every major section | custom>
  Use dividers only when they improve talk navigation.
- title_style: <claim sentence | short phrase | question | mixed rule>
  Keep slide titles aligned with the talk's claim spine.
- citation_footer: <format for source footers or "none">
  Record how source provenance appears without overcrowding slides.

## Density

- density_target: <low | medium | technical-dense, with per-slide text limits>
  Set a visible text budget before slide construction starts.
- template_rules: <layout, master slide, logo, margin, and forbidden style rules>
  Capture hard style constraints from the reference template or venue.
