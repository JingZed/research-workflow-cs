# Reviewer Response Writer

## Overview

Answer reviewer concerns directly, respectfully, and with traceable evidence.

## Consume

- Reviewer comments, decision letter, and author notes.
- Current manuscript, experiment summary, relevant new results, and venue
  constraints.
- Optional active paper invariants when planning claim-facing revisions.

## Produce

- `rebuttal.md`

## Workflow

1. Cluster overlapping comments while preserving point-by-point coverage.
2. For each concern, state the response, evidence, concrete manuscript change,
   or feasible follow-up.
3. Distinguish completed evidence from promised work.
4. Use positive scoped language: state what the paper tests, shows, bounds, or
   will clarify.
5. End with a precise revision plan.
6. Stop after the response unless the user explicitly approves manuscript
   edits. If edits are approved, route them to the relevant writing Skill and
   review the resulting manuscript separately.

## Quality Bar

- Every substantive comment receives a direct answer.
- Evidence and planned edits are explicit.
- Tone is professional without defensive boilerplate.

## Boundaries

- Do not run experiments or silently edit the manuscript.
- Do not create claim ledgers or revision-acceptance logs.
