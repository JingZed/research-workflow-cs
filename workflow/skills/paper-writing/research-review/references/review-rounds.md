# Deep Review Round Pattern

## Briefing

Include:

- target and desired decision;
- central claims and contribution;
- method and evaluation design;
- strongest positive, negative, null, and missing evidence;
- known limitations and closest work;
- resource constraints;
- exact questions for the reviewer.

Remove secrets, local paths, private identities, and unpublished exact details
unless their transmission to the named reviewer is authorized.

## Round 1

Ask for:

- strongest rejection argument;
- logical or methodological gaps;
- missing controls and experiments;
- prior-work and positioning risk;
- narrative weaknesses;
- confidence and evidence behind each criticism.

## Round 2

For each material concern, present the exact evidence or counterargument. Ask:

- what evidence would falsify the concern;
- the minimum credible experiment or analysis;
- whether a narrower claim survives;
- which criticism is highest priority.

## Round 3

Request:

- ranked minimum experiment package with rough cost;
- claim-to-evidence matrix under positive, negative, ambiguous, and blocked
  outcomes;
- defensible story alternatives;
- optional mock review and score;
- unresolved questions that remain genuinely decision-changing.
