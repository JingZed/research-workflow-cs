# Results Sufficiency Rubric

## Claim-Evidence Row

For each planned claim record:

- claim text and scope;
- required comparison or observation;
- supporting result IDs and source locators;
- baseline/control parity;
- sample, seed, uncertainty, effect size, and units;
- relevant negative, failed, or conflicting evidence;
- status: `supported`, `weak`, `missing`, `conflicting`, or
  `technically blocked`;
- smallest evidence addition or narrower wording that resolves the row.

## Minimum-Evidence Questions

- Is the central comparison fair and reproducible?
- Are the claimed direction and magnitude visible under uncertainty?
- Are required baselines and sanity controls present?
- Could a known confound explain the result?
- Are failures, nulls, and exclusions accounted for?
- Does the claim stay within tested tasks, datasets, scales, seeds, and settings?
- Would the conclusion remain useful if the effect weakens?

Do not turn optional completeness into a blocker. Require additional evidence
only when it changes the support status of a planned claim.
